Privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Creekside Plumbing & Gas (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can access or correct it.

What we collect

  • Quote requests: the situation you pick, property type, suburb, preferred time, your name and phone number.
  • Job applications: your name, phone number, licence details and experience.
  • When you contact us or we do work for you: details you give us by phone, email or social media, the job address, access instructions, and photos of the job.
  • Website use: information collected by cookies and similar technology, such as pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location, how you found the site, and whether you submitted a form or tapped our phone number.

How we use it

  • to call you back, quote, schedule and carry out work;
  • for invoicing, warranty claims and records we’re required to keep, such as compliance certificates for gas and plumbing work;
  • to consider job applications;
  • to understand how the website is used and measure our advertising.

We don’t sell your information, and we won’t add you to marketing lists or make sales calls.

Who we share it with

We only share personal information when we need to:

  • service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting, form and email delivery, and accounting software;
  • Google, which provides our website analytics and advertising measurement (Google Analytics and Google Ads);
  • government regulators, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers, including Google, may store information outside Australia.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics and Google Ads cookies to measure visits and see which ads lead to enquiries. These don’t tell us who you are. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or manage ad personalisation in Google’s My Ad Center.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. We keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires, then delete or de-identify it.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us using the details below. If you have a concern about how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first and we’ll respond as soon as we can. If you’re not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
